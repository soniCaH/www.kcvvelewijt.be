5 · Q8: text that is not a label, now vs the rule

Every other place that sets its own tracking (scheurkalender is out of scope). Rule is the tracking of the nearest step: display steps carry −0.025em or −0.035em, mono and body steps carry 0em, an uppercase mono label takes text-label's value. Only the tracking changes here; the size stays. Serif rows use a Georgia stand-in for Freight.

Tailwind's stock names resolve to: tracking-wide 0.025em · tracking-wider 0.05em · tracking-widest 0.1em · tracking-tight −0.025em. They are counted in section 1 under those values.
