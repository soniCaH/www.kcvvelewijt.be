# Colors

All color values live in `src/app/globals.css` inside `@theme {}`.
The swatches below read directly from the live CSS custom properties — no hard-coded duplicates.

The legacy `--color-kcvv-*`, `--color-green--*` digital palette was retired in
the redesign (#2154). The site now ships a single retro cream-on-ink surface
vocabulary — the tokens below are the canonical set.

---

## Cream Surface (page backgrounds)

The default page surface. Layered cards step down from `cream` → `cream-soft`
→ `cream-deep`.

<SwatchRow items={[

  { name: 'cream-soft (layered cards)', variable: '--color-cream-soft', hex: '#ede8da' },
  { name: 'cream-deep (deepest section bg)', variable: '--color-cream-deep', hex: '#e1d7bf' },
]} />

`manila` is not part of that step-down — it is a separate, non-semantic warm
paper stock that **chapters a long index page, once per page** (#2614). It is
never a step-down (`cream-soft` keeps that job) and never one of the three
soft status tints below, which stay reserved to the Alert variants.

## Ink (text + dark interludes)

`ink` is the primary text colour on cream and the fill for dark interlude
panels. `ink-muted` is the quiet voice: bylines / metadata. Two tiers, not
three — `ink-soft` is a surface only, never a text colour (DESIGN.md → The
Two-Tier Text Rule). On dark, `cream` is the voice and `cream-quiet` (cream at
70% alpha, never on jersey-deep) the quiet voice.

]} />

## Jersey (brand green)

The brand green. `jersey` is decorative/accent only (never text on cream);
`jersey-deep` carries everything that is text — headings, primary CTAs and
inline prose links alike; `jersey-bright` is green text on dark interludes;
`jersey-deep-dark` is the desaturated deep-green used in dark gradients.

## No neutral greys

There is no grey in this palette. `gray-100` was the last one — the surface
under the homepage news grid and banner strips — and #2342 removed it along
with the `white` / `gray-100` options on `SectionBg`; those sections are
`transparent` now, so the page cream shows through. `foundation-gray-light` and
the four `table-*` tokens went earlier, alongside the blurred shadow family;
none of them had a consumer.

A section surface is cream, a step of cream, or ink. If one ever needs to step
down from the page without going dark, add `cream-soft` to `SectionBg` rather
than reviving a grey.

The table above is the complete shadow vocabulary. Every entry has a `0` blur
radius — depth in this system is drawn, not simulated. The pre-redesign blurred
family (`--shadow-sm`, `--shadow-DEFAULT`, `--shadow-md`, `--shadow-lg`,
`--shadow-card-hover`, `--shadow-input`, `--shadow-input-focus`, `--shadow-soft`)
and the asymmetric `--shadow-photo-tape` / `-lift` pair were all removed: none of
them had a consumer. `<TapedFigure>` declares no shadow of its own — it delegates
to `<TapedCard>`.
